Questions

Frequently asked

Can I order online?

Yes, for pickup. Build your list, open it and tap “Send order for pickup”. We'll pack it and this page shows when your order is ready — keep it open or come back to it. We don't deliver yet; for large or catering orders, call us.

How do I know if something is in stock?

Every product shows whether it's in stock, running low, or sold out. If an item is sold out, we suggest a close alternative. Stock changes daily, so call ahead if you need something specific.

When does fresh produce arrive?

Fresh vegetables and fruit arrive several times a week, and new snacks, frozen items and seasonal puja kits come in regularly. Check the “Fresh arrivals” tab to see what's just come in.

Do you carry festival and pooja items?

Yes — incense in several fragrances, Karva Chauth kits and other pooja items, especially in the weeks before a festival.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking is available in the shopping center lot right outside the store.

Can you special-order a product?

Often, yes. Ask at the counter or call (410) 203-2525 and we'll check with our suppliers.

Will my shopping list be saved?

Your list stays saved in this browser on this device. To take it with you, use Print or Share from the list panel.
